How to use these guides

Each guide starts with a quick-reference table so you can look up a single parameter fast, then goes deeper into each group of controls with a plain-language explanation, a real-world analogy, and a callout for anything that behaves differently than you'd expect coming from the other shader. You don't need to read them front to back — jump to the section you need.

Written for lookdev and lighting artists switching render engines. No prior shader-writing knowledge assumed.
